# How To Use the Note Analyzer

You do not need complicated commands.

Give Claude the course material and tell it what you want done.

## Basic Notes

> Analyze this material and turn it into organized notes using my note-taking system.

## Messy Notes

> These notes are jumbled. Analyze them, group related ideas, fix the organization, preserve important information, and turn them into clean study notes.

## PowerPoint

> Analyze this PowerPoint and turn it into study-ready notes. Do not simply copy the slides. Analyze the text, diagrams, charts, and meaningful images. Combine related slides and simplify long explanations without removing important information.

## PowerPoint With Test Information

> Before analyzing the presentation, treat the following as confirmed TEST PRIORITY:
>
> - [Topic]
> - [Topic]
> - [Topic]
>
> Pay extra attention to definitions, formulas, processes, examples, comparisons, and important details related to those topics. Then analyze the full presentation normally.

## Lecture Transcript

> Turn this lecture transcript into organized notes. Remove filler and repeated speech, but preserve professor explanations, examples, definitions, and anything that appears important.

## Clean Existing Notes

> Analyze these existing notes. Fix unclear organization, repeated information, awkward wording, and missing structure without changing the meaning.

## Study Focus

> Analyze these notes and identify what I should focus on most when studying. Keep confirmed test material separate from material that only appears possibly testable.

## Important Rule

Do not tell Claude that a topic is confirmed test material unless you actually know that from the professor, study guide, student, or supplied material.
